# 📖 Project Overview

Businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day. However, without effective reporting and visualization, valuable insights often remain hidden.

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Power BI can transform raw retail sales data into meaningful business intelligence through interactive dashboards, KPI reporting, and data visualization.

The dashboard enables business managers to monitor sales performance, evaluate revenue growth, compare regional performance, identify high-performing products, and support strategic decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Retail organizations often struggle to answer important business questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which region contributes the most sales?
- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which product category performs best?
- Which KPIs should management monitor?

Without an interactive dashboard, answering these questions often requires time-consuming manual analysis.

This project provides a centralized Business Intelligence solution to address these challenges.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis produced several important insights:

- ✅ Technology generates the highest sales.
- ✅ West Region contributes the highest revenue.
- ✅ Revenue increased significantly between **2015 and 2017**.
- ✅ Canon products rank among the highest-selling products.

These insights support strategic business planning and executive decision-making.
